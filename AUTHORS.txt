# Lankaverse Sans — Authors

Lankaverse Sans is a derivative work based on the following typefaces by
the Indian Type Foundry, each released under the SIL Open Font License:

- Hind
- Hind Madurai
- Hind Colombo

Original design and engineering (Hind / Hind Madurai / Hind Colombo):
- Jyotish Sonowal
- Manushi Parikh
- Satya Rajpurohit
- and other contributors at Indian Type Foundry (https://www.indiantypefoundry.com/)

Lankaverse Sans adaptation, Sinhala and Tamil development, and ongoing
maintenance:
- Pathum Egodawatta (https://pathumego.com/) — Lankaverse Media project, Mooniak
